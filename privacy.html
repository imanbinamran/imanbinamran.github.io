Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Claude Code Integration ("the App") handles information when you sign in with your Google account.

1. Information the App accesses

With your permission, granted through Google OAuth, the App may access:

  • Gmail: email messages, metadata, labels, drafts and filters, and the ability to send email on your behalf.
  • Google Calendar: calendars and events, including the ability to create, edit, delete and respond to events.
  • Basic profile: your email address, to identify the connected account.

2. How the information is used

Data is used only to carry out actions you explicitly request through Claude Code, such as finding an email, drafting a reply or scheduling a meeting. The App does not use your data for advertising, profiling or any unrelated purpose.

3. Storage

The App runs locally on your own device. OAuth tokens are stored only on that device. The App does not operate its own servers and does not copy your Gmail or Calendar data to any database it controls.

When you ask Claude Code to work with your data, the content needed to complete your request is processed by Anthropic's Claude AI service under Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

4. Sharing

The App does not sell, rent or share your Google user data with third parties, except as described in Section 3 to fulfil your own requests, or where required by law.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the locally stored token files removes all stored credentials from your device.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
